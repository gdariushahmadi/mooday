## YYYY-MM-DD - [Loading State Indicators]
**Learning:** Adding a spinner to loading states in form submission buttons makes the "submitting" state much more clear and intuitive. It's a common micro-UX pattern to show both a disabled state and an animating visual indicator (like a spinner) rather than just changing the button text.
**Action:** When implementing form submission buttons, always include an animating spinner (like a material symbol with a spin animation) alongside the loading text when the form is in a submitting state.
